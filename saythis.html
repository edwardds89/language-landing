Every place here has agreed to one thing: if you show your pin, whoever speaks Italian will speak it with you. The times below are when Italian-speaking staff are usually in — usually, not always. If you get there and nobody does, tell us after your tap and we'll fix that venue or drop it.

What you get

1
Nothing to sign up for No app, no account, no password. You tap the sticker, answer three questions, and that's the whole thing.
2
Your visits are counted Every tap during the October window is logged against the place you were in. That's what tells us which venues actually work.
3
Founding members come first at launch Whoever showed up in October gets the founding price for life, early access, and the enamel pin — handed out when we launch, not before. We'd rather promise less now and deliver it than the other way round.

After you tap — three quick questions

1
Was there anyone to speak Italian with? Yes, and I spoke it · There was, but we ended up in English · There was nobody. The third answer is the one we most need — it's how a venue gets fixed or dropped.
2
How did it go? A line or two, in English or in Italian — whichever comes out. Optional, but it's the part we actually read.
3
Would you go back? Yes · Maybe · No. One tap and you're done — no sign-in, no forms, nothing to fill in.

How a visit works

1
Walk in and show your pin. It tells them you're learning, and asks them to keep answering in Italian — or to point you to someone who can.
2
Order in Italian. Use the phrases. Getting it wrong is part of it — that's why you showed the pin first.
3
At the till, tap your phone on the sticker. While you pay. Hold the back of your phone against the LanguagePin sticker for a second. If you don't tap, the visit didn't happen — the tap is the only record there is. It's also where you tell us how it went, including if there was nobody to speak Italian with. No NFC on your phone? Scan the QR on the same sticker.
